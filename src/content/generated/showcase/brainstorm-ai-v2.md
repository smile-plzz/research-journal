# AI Ideation Studio 🚀

Welcome to the **AI Ideation Studio**, an advanced collaborative think-tank where you assemble bespoke panels of elite AI experts to pitch, debate, roast, and refine your next big ideas. 

---

## ✨ Features

- **Dynamic Preset Lab Chambers**: Instantiate preset environments instantly—such as *Startup Launchpad*, *Ethical Tech Summit*, *Architecture Review*, *Viral Marketing Lab*, and *The Roast Tank*.
- **Generative Expert Inception**: Describe any scenario, and the AI will dynamically spawn the perfect roster of customized experts matching that niche.
- **Bespoke Character Customization**: Add, remove, or customize any system prompt manually. Choose from predefined templates like *Lead Developer*, *Aggressive VC*, *UX Guru*, *Growth Hacker*, and *Ethical Critic*.
- **Interactive Multi-Agent Debates**: Control the synergy level using the *Debate Intensity Parameters* (Structured Debate, Dynamic Panel, Synergy Chaos).
- **Collapsible Sidebar Workspace**: Maximise your workspace. Toggle the active panelists panel out of view to focus purely on the discussion stream.
- **Skip Turn & Let Panel Brainstorm**: Feel like listening instead of talking? Use the "Skip Turn" trigger to let the expert panel brainstorm, debate, and bounce concepts off one another without requiring manual input.
- **Smart Focus-Scroll Positioning**: The discussion stream automatically scrolls to the bottom during live streaming, with a fallback interactive "New responses" snap badge if you scroll up to read.
- **Export & Transcripts**: Cleanly export complete markdown transcripts of your brainstorming session directly with a single click.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React 18 with Vite, TypeScript.
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons.
- **Animation**: Framer Motion (`motion/react`).
- **Markdown Parsing**: `react-markdown`.
- **Backend & AI Routing**: Full-stack Node.js server powered by Mistral AI / OpenRouter via a streaming Server-Sent Events (SSE) connector.

---

## 🏃 Getting Started

1. Select your target Preset Scene or describe a custom scenario.
2. Polish the active panel members inside the **Roster Lab**. Add custom experts or select predefined specialists.
3. Choose your desired crosstalk level.
4. Launch the session!
5. Type your ideas or click **Skip Turn** to watch the AI minds collaborate in real-time.
