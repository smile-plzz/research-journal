# Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare & Modern Warfare Remastered Academic Research Project

## Project Overview
This repository hosts a comprehensive, interdisciplinary academic research project and critical game studies monograph titled:
**"Modern Warfare Before the Modern War: Narrative Construction, Military Representation, Political Ideology, and Player Experience in Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare (2007) and Modern Warfare Remastered (2016)"**

- **Primary Discipline:** Game Studies
- **Secondary Disciplines:** Military Studies, Media Studies, Political Science, Narrative Studies, Cultural Studies, Psychology of Interactive Media, International Relations, and Digital Humanities
- **Citation Style:** APA 7th Edition
- **Target Manuscript Length:** 9,000–11,000 words (excluding references and appendices)
- **Primary Research Object:** Complete single-player campaign of *Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare* (2007, Infinity Ward)
- **Comparative Research Object:** *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare Remastered* (2016, Raven Software)
- **Secondary Research Object:** Original and remastered multiplayer systems

## Methodology & Workflow Architecture
Research follows the procedural specification of the [Academic Research Skills (ARS)](https://github.com/imbad0202/academic-research-skills) framework across 10 structured stages:
1. **Stage 1: Deep Research (Full Mode)** — Literature discovery, research question formulation, evidence matrix, and synthesis.
2. **Stage 2: Academic Paper Drafting (Full Mode)** — Rigorous manuscript drafting and structural articulation.
3. **Stage 2.5: Integrity Verification** — Reference audits, anti-fabrication checks, and claim-to-evidence alignment.
4. **Stage 3: Multi-Perspective Peer Review** — 5-seat review panel (Journal-Fit, Methodology, Domain Expertise, Cross-Disciplinary, and Devil's Advocate).
5. **Stage 4: Manuscript Revision** — Systematic revision roadmap execution.
6. **Stage 3': Focused Re-Review** — Verification of revision compliance.
7. **Stage 4.5: Final Integrity Verification** — Zero-tolerance reference and fact check.
8. **Stage 5: Finalization & Deliverable Generation** — Full manuscript assembly and supplementary exports.
9. **Stage 6: Process Record** — Complete audit trail of research execution.

## Repository Layout
- `research/call-of-duty-4-modern-warfare/` — Complete suite of 21 formal academic deliverables (00 to 20 + `references.bib`), including `20_MISSION_AMBIENCE_AND_PSYCHOLOGICAL_DEEP_DIVE.md`.
- `dist/` — Distribution publication package:
  - `dist/Modern_Warfare_Academic_Monograph.docx`: APA 7th Edition formatted Word document.
  - `dist/Modern_Warfare_Academic_Monograph.pdf`: 19-page publication-quality academic PDF monograph.
- `dashboard/` — Interactive web research application:
  - `dashboard/index.html`: Standalone single-file visual research portal with 21-mission matrix, character dossier, and 2007 vs 2016 comparative engine.
- `scripts/` — Automated build and export pipeline:
  - `scripts/export_manuscript.py`: Python script converting Markdown to publication-grade DOCX and PDF.
- `dev-log.md` — Chronological log of execution steps and milestones.
- `context.md` — Project goals, technical constraints, theoretical stack, and parameters.
- `CHANGELOG.md` — Versioned updates and audit notes.
