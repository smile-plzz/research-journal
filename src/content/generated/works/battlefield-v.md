# Battlefield V (2018) — War Stories Campaign Academic Research Project

## Project Overview
This repository hosts a comprehensive, interdisciplinary academic research paper and critical game studies investigation into the single-player campaign of *Battlefield V* (DICE/Electronic Arts, 2018).

The campaign comprises:
1. **Prologue:** *My Country Calling* (London 1939, Narvik 1940, Tobruk 1941, Kasserine Pass 1942, Hamburg 1943, Nijmegen Bridge 1944)
2. **War Story One:** *Under No Flag* (North Africa, 1942; Billy Bridger, George Mason, British Special Boat Section)
3. **War Story Two:** *Nordlys* (Occupied Norway, 1943; Solveig Fia Bjørnstad, Astrid Bjørnstad, heavy water sabotage)
4. **War Story Three:** *Tirailleur* (Southern France, 1944; Deme Cissé, Idrissa, Senegalese Tirailleurs, Operation Dragoon)
5. **War Story Four:** *The Last Tiger* (Ruhr/Rhine, Germany, 1945; Peter Müller, Kertz, Hartmann, Schröder, Tiger 237)

## Research Dimensions
1. **Historical Dimension:** Real historical events, military formations, operations, and geopolitical contexts.
2. **Narrative Dimension:** Narrative architecture, character arcs, dialogue, interpersonal relationships, and ethical resolutions.
3. **Psychological Dimension:** Fear, trauma, brotherhood, guilt, disillusionment, survival, and moral dissonance.
4. **Interactive Dimension:** Mechanics of destruction, stealth, mobility (skiing), combat, vehicular armour simulation, and procedural rhetoric.
5. **Critical & Cultural Dimension:** Colonial memory, racial hierarchy, historical erasure (*blanchiment*), gendered resistance, and the historiography of the Wehrmacht (*Clean Wehrmacht* myth vs. complicity).

## Research Pipeline
Conducted following the Academic Research Skills (ARS) framework (v3.24.1 pipeline specification):
- **Stage 1: Deep Research** (Literature discovery, source verification, annotated bibliography, evidence synthesis)
- **Stage 2: Academic Paper Drafting** (Analytical monographs for all campaign segments, cross-cutting matrices, full manuscript draft)
- **Stage 2.5: Mandatory Integrity Verification** (7-mode AI failure audit, citation provenance check)
- **Stage 3: Multi-Perspective Peer Review** (5-seat panel: Journal-Fit, Methodology, Domain/Game Studies, Interdisciplinary/History, Devil's Advocate)
- **Stage 4: Manuscript Revision** (Point-by-point rebuttal, revision delta report, updated text)
- **Stage 3': Re-Review & Traceability Matrix**
- **Stage 4.5: Final Integrity Verification** (Mode 2 deep audit)
- **Stage 5: Finalization & Formatting** (APA 7th edition, publication Markdown, DOCX compilation, BibTeX)
- **Stage 6: Process Record & Collaboration Reflection**

## Target Output
- Manuscript length: 9,000–11,000 words (excluding references and appendices)
- Deliverables: All 20 canonical analytical documents and matrices.
